## Description
What if a blockchain rewarded you for keeping old computers alive instead of replacing them with newer mining hardware?

This explainer breaks down RustChain's Proof of Antiquity model: “one CPU, one vote,” hardware-age multipliers, the six-check hardware fingerprint used to distinguish physical machines from virtualized farms, and the tenure rule that increases weight for sustained participation.

It also covers the important limitation: a PowerPC G4 is not faster than a modern workstation. RustChain is deliberately optimizing for verified physical identity, hardware diversity and preservation rather than raw compute throughput.
